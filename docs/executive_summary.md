# Executive Summary

## Objective
Understand how SaaS users convert, engage, retain and contribute to recurring revenue.

## Management View

The analysis is organized around four product-growth questions:

### Conversion
Where do users drop out between signup, trial and paid subscription?

### Engagement
How frequently do active customers return to the product?

### Retention
Which signup cohorts continue using the product after acquisition?

### Revenue
How much monthly revenue comes from first-time customers versus existing customers?

## Decision Framework

Use the dashboard to:
1. Monitor the headline KPI trend.
2. Locate the weakest funnel or cohort area.
3. Segment the issue by channel or customer attributes.
4. Form a driver hypothesis.
5. Define an action or investigation.
6. Monitor the relevant KPI after intervention.

## Important Interpretation Rule
Observed differences should be treated as descriptive unless the underlying experiment or observational design supports causal inference.
